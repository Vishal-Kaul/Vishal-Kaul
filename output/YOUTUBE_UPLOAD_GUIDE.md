Video file: **`top5_wildest_football_moments_commentary.mp4`** (final version with commentary, vertical 1080×1920, 54 seconds, so it counts as a Short)

---

## 1. Upload steps (phone, in the YouTube app)

1. Open the YouTube app, tap **+**, then **Upload a video**, and pick the file.
2. Leave it uncut; it's already under 60 seconds.
3. **Don't add a sound.** The video already has music, commentary and sound effects, and adding a sound would split your revenue with the music owners.
4. Tap **Next** and fill in the details from section 2.
5. **Select audience:** choose *"No, it's not made for kids"*. If you choose "made for kids", comments are turned off and the video gets recommended to fewer people.
6. **Visibility:** choose **Public** (or **Schedule**, see section 4).
7. Tap **Upload Short**.

From a computer: go to studio.youtube.com, click **Create**, then **Upload videos**. The fields are the same. On a computer you can also add **Tags** under "Show more".

## 4. When to post
- Football fans are most active **in the evenings and at weekends**, and around big matches (Champions League nights, El Clásico, Premier League weekends).
- After about a week, open **YouTube Studio**, go to **Analytics**, then **Audience**, and check *"When your viewers are on YouTube"*. Post at those times from then on.

---

## 5. Getting engagement in the first hour
How a Short does in its first hour decides how widely YouTube shows it.

1. **Pin a comment** right after you upload, for example *"Which number was the wildest? 1–5 👇"*. A question like this gets people commenting.
2. **Reply to every comment** in the first hour.
3. Share it to your Instagram Story, WhatsApp status, and football groups. Views that come from outside YouTube help too.
4. **Link it in Shorts that are already doing well:** in another Short, tap **Related video** and choose this one.
5. Add it to a **"Football Top 5"** playlist on your channel.

---

## 6. What helps a channel grow
- **Post regularly**, ideally 1 Short a day. Uploading often matters more than any single tag.
- **Make this into a series**: "Top 5 wildest saves", "Top 5 Messi moments", "Top 5 fails" and so on. A series brings viewers back.
- **Check Analytics**, then **"Viewed vs swiped away"**. If more than about 30% of people swipe away, make the first 1–2 seconds more exciting.
- Tags and hashtags matter less than **people watching to the end and watching again**. A tight video under 60 seconds helps with both.

## ⚠️ Important: copyright
These clips are match footage owned by leagues and broadcasters (UEFA and Premier League footage, the Man City logo), so YouTube may put a **Content ID claim** on the video. This is common for football Shorts, and usually it means:
- The video stays up, but any **ad money goes to the owner of the footage**. This happens most often.
- Sometimes the video is **blocked in some countries** or **muted**.
- A **copyright strike** is less likely, but can happen. Three strikes and YouTube deletes the channel.

To lower the risk, add your own commentary or voiceover, keep each clip short, and use clips from creators who allow reuse. No upload method or tag guarantees ranking or views. What gets a video recommended is the content plus how quickly people engage with it.
