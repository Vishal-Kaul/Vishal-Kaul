Everything below is ready to copy into **YouTube Studio → Customization** and **Settings**.
Text in `[brackets]` is yours to fill in.

---

## 4. Upload defaults (Settings → Upload defaults)
These pre-fill every new upload, which saves time on each Short.

**Description template:**
```

[Debate question] Comment below 👇
Subscribe for more football Shorts ⚽

Music: [track] by [artist] (Pixabay)

#football #soccer #shorts
```

**Default tags:**
```
football, soccer, football shorts, football skills, messi, ronaldo, messi vs ronaldo, wildest football moments, top 5 football moments, football edits
```

**Other defaults:**
- Visibility: Public
- Category: Sports
- Comments: On (sorted by Top)
- Language: English
- Not made for kids
- Altered or synthetic content: **Yes**, as long as you use the AI commentary voice

## 9. Posting plan
- **How often:** 1 Short a day if you can, or at least 3–4 a week. Posting regularly matters more than any tag.
- **When:** evenings in your audience's time zone, and around big matches (Champions League nights, El Clásico, derby weekends).
- **First hour after posting:** pin the debate comment, reply to every comment, and share the link to Instagram, WhatsApp and football groups.
- **Weekly check:** YouTube Studio → Analytics → see which Shorts kept people watching (look at "viewed vs swiped away"), then make more like those.

## ⚠️ Keep the channel safe
- Match clips belong to leagues and broadcasters, so some Shorts may get **Content ID claims**. A claim usually means the ad money goes to the owner of the footage; it isn't a strike.
- To qualify for the **YouTube Partner Program**, your channel needs to show **your own added value**: commentary, ranking, edits and debate questions. Keep doing that on every video, and never upload plain re-uploads of match clips.
- Always credit the music, and mark the AI voice as altered or synthetic content.
